# Italian frequency lexicon — data license / イタリア語頻度辞書のライセンス

The file `Sources/KanaKanjiConverterModule/Resources/it_words.txt` (Copaky fork addition) is a
word-frequency list derived from three corpora of the **Leipzig Corpora Collection**, blended and
filtered by the Copaky project:

- `ita_news_2022_300K` · `ita-it_web-public_2019_2019_300K` · `ita_mixed-typical_2017_300K`

**License of the source corpora: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).**
Attribution: © Universität Leipzig / Sächsische Akademie der Wissenschaften / InfAI —
https://wortschatz.uni-leipzig.de ("All corpora provided for download are licensed under CC BY.")

Reference: D. Goldhahn, T. Eckart & U. Quasthoff: *Building Large Monolingual Dictionaries at the
Leipzig Corpora Collection: From 100 to 200 Languages.* In: Proceedings of the 8th International
Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC'12), 2012.

A small supplementary set of interpersonal/everyday words (greetings, courtesy, messaging terms)
was authored directly by the Copaky project and is released under the repository's MIT license.
